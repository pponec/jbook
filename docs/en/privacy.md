* **Legal basis:** Legitimate interest of the controller and the hosting provider in ensuring secure website operation (according to Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f) GDPR).

Log data is managed by GitHub, and the operator of this website does not have direct access to it. For more information, please see the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

This website **does not use** any marketing, tracking, or analytical third-party cookies (such as Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel).

This data remains in your browser, is not sent anywhere, and is not used to identify you personally.
